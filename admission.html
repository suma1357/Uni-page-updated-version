BE PREPARED

Admission requirements

Requirements vary by program and applicant category. The following checklist is a general example, not an official admission policy.

  • Completed application form
  • Previous academic certificates and transcripts
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Valid identification and contact information
  • Additional documents required by the chosen program

Need help with your application?

The Admission Office can guide applicants through program selection and document preparation.
